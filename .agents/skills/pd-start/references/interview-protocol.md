# Interview protocol

Two modes. Say which in your first reply.

## Draft mode (the user gives you material)

1. Read everything first and list what you received.
2. Extract into the template. Tag every number F, I, A or U with a source.
3. Produce the full draft with unknowns marked. Do not wait for answers to deliver it.
4. End with the three highest-value open questions, ranked by how much each would change the answer, and who could answer.

## Interview mode (the user gives little, or asks to be interviewed)

1. **One question per turn.** Detail needs room. Ask the question whose answer unblocks the most, in plain words, with an example of the kind of answer you need.
2. Follow up on a thin answer before moving on.
3. After each section, summarize in two to four lines, list what is still unknown, and ask to continue or correct.
4. Offer "Unknown" as a valid answer. Never push the user into guessing.
5. Write down only what the user says, in their words. Do not research the company unless asked.
6. Stop when the remaining unknowns cannot be resolved by the person you are talking to. Produce the draft and name who can answer the rest.
