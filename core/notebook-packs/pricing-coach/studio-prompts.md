# Studio prompts: pricing coach

- **Quiz:** "Write 10 questions on the pricing spine and the operating rules. Include three about what the pricing desk must not decide."
- **Flashcards:** "Make flashcards for the nine granted rights and what each lets a customer do."
- **Report:** "Explain the difference between list price and deal price, and where the account enters. One page."
- **Audio overview:** "Walk a new analyst through one week on the pricing desk: daily, weekly and monthly rhythms."
- **Slide deck:** "Six slides on how we price an add-on for an account that has an anchor GDS deal."
- **Mind map:** "Map the eleven stages of the pricing spine and the skill that runs each."
