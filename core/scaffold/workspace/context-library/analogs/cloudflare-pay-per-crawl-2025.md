# Cloudflare pay per crawl, 2025

Facts as reported (F, one secondary source):
- Publishers set one flat per-request price for crawlers not allowed in for free, and can admit chosen crawlers at no charge.
- A crawler either sends payment intent in its request headers and receives the content, or receives a "402 Payment Required" response with pricing.
- Described at publication as a "private beta experiment" and "still in its very early stages".

What it suggests for our work (I): machine access can carry a price per request that the machine can read before it spends. Whether a travel data buyer would accept the same is untested. A price shown in the response is a way to put a metered price in front of an automated caller.

Re-check: a beta at the publication date; its status may have changed.
