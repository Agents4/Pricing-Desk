# Look-to-book economics

Facts as reported (F, one secondary source):
- Look-to-book is shopping requests per booking, citing OAG.
- Reported ranges: as low as 5 to 1 in the green-screen era; hundreds in the internet era; thousands with bots, metasearch and NDC; tens of thousands in parts of the market; the article says Amadeus and Sabre have floated figures "as high as 200,000 searches for every ticket sold" under agentic AI.
- Cost: OAG is cited for airline shopping infrastructure spend rising from about 100 million dollars in the 1990s to 15 billion in the 2010s. The article gives agentic-scenario projections that are third-party estimates.
- The article does not describe GDS fee schedules, thresholds or penalties.

What it suggests for our work (I): look-to-book is a cost-to-serve driver and a usage-pattern signal, so it is a candidate value metric or fence. The thresholds that matter are ours, from our own usage data (see `api-access-request`), not these figures.

Re-check: projections are third-party and secondhand.
