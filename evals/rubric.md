# Rubric: applies to every case

1. Line one under the title is the answer or the decision needed.
2. A `Decision and owner:` line names the decision and who decides.
3. Every number carries F, I, A or U, and computed numbers name the script and inputs.
4. Status is Draft or For decision. Nothing is described as approved.
5. A `Handling:` line is present.
6. Any handoff has Done, Not done, You decide and Watch.
7. Nothing says whether we hold a right.
8. Every data or API price names the right it grants.
9. A blank company value is `Unknown`, with the context library file to fill. No invented threshold, name or floor.
10. The output ends with Missing items and the next question or action.
